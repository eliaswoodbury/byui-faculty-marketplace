# BYU–Idaho faculty decision-risk cards

Use these short cards when a faculty member makes a consequential choice. Explain the risk in ordinary language and ask what the person would do next.

## Before connecting

The plugin may read information using the connected account and return some of it to the assistant. Check that the account, course, term, and tool are approved. Never paste a token into chat.

## Before defining an audience

A rule is being turned into a list of people. Confirm the course, section, active enrollment, threshold, dates, AND/OR choice, missing-data handling, and exact IDs. Do not use the plugin to infer who is lazy, dishonest, at risk, or in need of discipline.

## Before approving an audience

Audience approval permits the next step; it is not approval to send or save. Review the complete list, exclusions, counts, evaluation time, and uncertainty. A changed rule or stale list requires a fresh review.

## Before drafting or sending

Remove unsupported claims, sensitive details, stigmatizing language, wrong names, and inaccessible links. Audience approval and final-message approval are separate decisions.

## Before an external action

Confirm the destination, recipients, final content, side effect, and expiry. Prefer a dry run. Cancel if anything is unclear.

## After an uncertain result

Stop and reconcile the uncertain item before retrying. Do not create a new preview and resend the whole audience. Sent is not the same as read.

## If a tool asks for more data

Ask why the data is needed, where it will go, who can see it, and whether the destination is approved. Do not send student context to unrelated search, email, analytics, documentation, or public tools.
